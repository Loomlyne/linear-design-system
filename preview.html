Typography

01

Poppins carries every heading, label, and body line. IBM Plex Mono, tabular figures only, carries every real number — invoice totals, KPI values, ledger columns — so amounts actually align instead of drifting the way Poppins' proportional digits would.

Color

02

Near-black neutrals carry the interface. One accent — carried forward from the existing Invios brand gold, brightened for dark surfaces — is reserved for the primary action and nothing else. Success, warning, and destructive are separate hues; none of them borrow the accent's amber.

Components

03

Compact controls, mostly secondary or ghost — one restrained primary action per area. Status is always a colored dot plus a word, never color alone.
