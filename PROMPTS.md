# Invios — Full Client-Workflow OS: Claude Design Build Prompts

19 detailed, self-contained build prompts covering every screen of the full client-workflow OS (Dashboard, Invoices, Quotations, Clients, Settings, Auth, Proposals/E-Signature, Time Tracking, Projects & Tasks, Project Pages, Command Palette, Expenses/Income + Advanced Connectors, a Collapsible Icon-Rail Sidebar, two wiring/audit retrofits, a mobile-responsiveness audit, a mobile navbar redesign, and global income/expense logging with client-level earnings), all built against the [Linear Design System](./DESIGN.md).

## How to use these

1. Finish the "Set up your design system" form in Claude Design first — company name/blurb, the `linear-design-system` GitHub link, and the font files, per the setup answers already given.
2. Paste ONE prompt at a time into the chat, in the order below. Each is fully self-contained — it re-states the relevant design tokens and realistic content itself, so it doesn't depend on the others being pasted first, but building in this order matches how the features actually depend on each other product-wise (e.g. Time Tracking's "convert to invoice" flow assumes the Invoices editor already exists; step 13 assumes Invoices, Projects & Tasks, and Settings already exist; step 14 assumes Projects & Tasks, Project Pages, and step 13's Expenses & Income all already exist, since it's the nav shell connecting them; step 15 assumes 9, 10, 13, and 14 are all already built; step 16 assumes everything, 1 through 15, is already built, since it's a full audit pass; step 18 assumes steps 8, 11, 12, and 19 all need header real estate, since it's the header redesign that makes room for them; step 19 assumes steps 4, 13, 15, and 18 all already exist, since it extends all four).
3. Steps 1–12 were generated, adversarially critiqued against `DESIGN.md`, and fixed in a review pass; a final cross-module consistency scan found no drift across all 12 (same client names, invoice/quote/proposal numbering, and design tokens used identically everywhere they overlap). Steps 13–19 were added afterward, authored directly against the same design tokens and content bible for consistency.
4. **Steps 15, 16, and 17 are retrofits/audits, not new screens.** Step 15 wires 9, 10, and 13 into one tabbed Project Detail view. Step 16 is a full cross-screen audit — it found and fixes a real project-naming conflict (steps 9, 10, and 11 each independently invented different names for the same six projects) plus several missing navigation links (share/portal links, clickable rows, quick-create gaps). Step 17 is a mobile-responsiveness audit fixing screens that render desktop-width on real mobile viewports instead of collapsing correctly. **Paste 16 and 17 after everything else, even if earlier steps look fine** — what they fix isn't visible screen-by-screen, only when comparing screens against each other or testing at real mobile widths.
5. Step 18 redesigns the mobile header into two rows to fit the search trigger (step 11), the global timer widget (step 8, confirmed as shared chrome by step 16 fix #7), and the new Log Income/Expense action (step 19) — paste it after 17, before 19. Step 19 adds client-level earnings (extending step 4) and one shared Log Income/Expense dialog reachable from Client, Project, and Invoice screens plus a global entry point, with context-aware autofill when opened from inside a client/project/invoice.
6. Content bible used throughout, for reference: product name Invios, six recurring clients (Nour Al Fahad Trading LLC, Meridian Studio FZ, Castle Peak Retail, Halcyon Media Group, Bright Path Consulting, Ferrous & Oak Interiors), three team members (Sami Haddad/owner, Layla Nasser/designer, Omar Khalil/developer), "today" anchored to July 13, 2026. Canonical project names (see step 16 if steps 9–11 were built with different ones): Nour Al Fahad Trading LLC → "Inventory Portal," Meridian Studio FZ → "Homepage Redesign," Castle Peak Retail → "POS Rollout," Halcyon Media Group → "Brand Refresh," Bright Path Consulting → "Client Portal," Ferrous & Oak Interiors → "Catalog Website."
